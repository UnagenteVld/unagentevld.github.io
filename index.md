## 🛠️ Features Explained Simply

**Design Tokens** - These are like "recipe cards" for your brand. Developers use them to keep colors and fonts consistent across websites and apps. DesignHub generates these automatically in Tailwind CSS, CSS, and JSON formats.

**Accessibility Tools** - This ensures your brand works for everyone, including people with visual impairments. DesignHub checks:
- Color contrast (text readability)
- Text size recommendations
- Icon clarity
All against WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) standards.

**Mockup Generator** - No more complex design software. Choose a product photo (like a business card, coffee mug, or billboard) and DesignHub automatically places your logo and colors on it. Perfect for presentations.

**SVG Tools** - SVG is a professional image format that scales to any size without losing quality. DesignHub lets you create and edit SVGs with simple tools - no coding needed.
